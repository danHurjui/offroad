These .ttf files are bundled with the pdfmake npm package
(https://github.com/bpampuch/pdfmake) and are used here, unmodified, to
render diacritics (ă, â, î, ș, ț) in server-generated PDFs — the PDF
standard-14 fonts (Helvetica etc.) don't cover Romanian characters.
